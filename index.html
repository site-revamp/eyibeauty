What can I discover at EYI Beauty?
Explore skincare, makeup, bath and body products, and beauty tools through the existing online store.
Does EYI Beauty offer personal shopping?
Yes. EYI Beauty describes an independent personal-shopping and product-sourcing service for customers requesting particular beauty products.
How does the product-sourcing service work?
Customers can contact EYI Beauty with a product request to discuss the sourcing and purchasing arrangements. Availability and individual terms should be confirmed directly before proceeding.
Is EYI Beauty affiliated with the brands it features?
The website states that EYI Beauty operates independently of the featured beauty brands unless otherwise stated. Brand names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
