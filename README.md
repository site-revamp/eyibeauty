EYI Beauty Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
EYI Beauty is a UK-facing online beauty and cosmetics retailer founded in 2020. The business offers skincare, makeup, cosmetics, bath and body products, alongside an independent personal-shopping and international product-sourcing service.

The redesigned homepage focuses on creating a clean, beauty-focused shopping experience that improves product discovery, communicates the sourcing service and strengthens the website's overall retail identity.
2. Main Improvements
The redesign addresses three critical problems identified in the previous website.
1. Removal of Unrelated Casino and Gambling Content
- Replaced unrelated casino and gambling references with beauty-focused homepage content, navigation and metadata.
- Restored the homepage's relevance to beauty retail and its intended audience.
- Important: This homepage redesign does not remove casino pages or injected content from the live website. Separate website and server-level investigation and remediation are required.
2. Improved Product Discovery
- Introduced dedicated shopping pathways for skincare, makeup, and bath and body products.
- Improved category navigation and visual hierarchy.
- Made the extensive product offering easier for visitors to explore.
3. Greater Visibility for Personal Shopping and Product Sourcing
- Introduced a dedicated section highlighting the personal-shopping and product-sourcing service.
- Explained the independent nature of the service and its sourcing proposition.
- Added clearer enquiry pathways to help customers understand how to request assistance.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual identity: Applied EYI Beauty's pink brand colour (#E51B95), supported by black and white.
- Layout: Established a clearer homepage structure with more purposeful content sections.
- Navigation: Improved the organisation and presentation of product categories.
- User experience: Created clearer shopping and product-sourcing journeys.
- Consistency: Maintained a cohesive beauty-focused visual direction across the homepage.
- Brand presentation: Shifted the overall appearance towards a more polished beauty and cosmetics retail experience.
4. Implementation Note
The redesign addresses the three identified homepage problems through improved content relevance, product discovery and service visibility. The unrelated casino content found on the existing website remains a separate security and website-integrity concern requiring investigation beyond the homepage redesign.
